Registration

ruSMART 2026 Registration
Registration is required for participation in ruSMART 2026 and for the inclusion of accepted papers in the conference proceedings.
Important Requirement for Accepted Papers
One full registration is mandatory for each accepted paper to be included in the conference proceedings, even if the presenting author is a student.

Multi-Paper Discount Rules:
  • First paper → Full Fee (according to the category).
  • Second paper → 50% discount, provided that at least one author is common across the papers.
  • Third paper onwards → Full Fee (no further discount).
  • Exception: The 50% discount on the second paper is not applicable to Türkiye-based authors (Istanbul University, Istanbul Gelişim University, and other Türkiye institutions) and Abu Dhabi University authors, as they already receive special discounted rates.
Authors are requested to carefully calculate and pay the correct eligible fee. Incorrect fee payment may result in the paper not being processed further.
