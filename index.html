Welcome to ruSMART 2026

ruSMART 2026, The 19th International Conference on Internet of Things and Smart Spaces, will be held in Istanbul, Turkey, 23–25 November 2026, bringing together researchers, academics, industry experts, and practitioners working on the Internet of Things, smart spaces, and intelligent systems and applications.

The conference provides an international forum for exchanging new ideas, research results, and practical experience in the design, development, deployment, and evaluation of smart spaces and IoT-enabled systems.

The history of ruSMART dates back to 2008. Proceedings of ruSMART 2026 will be published in Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) (approved) and indexed by relevant databases. The peer-review process is managed through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT), provided for free by Microsoft.

Authors, researchers, students, and participants from around the world are warmly invited to join ruSMART 2026 in Istanbul.
