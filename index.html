>> About the conference

 

Conference will be hosted in Istanbul, Turkey, November 23-25, 2026



Welcome to The 19th International Conference on Internet of Things and Smart Spaces ruSMART 2026. ruSMART 2026 proceedings will be published in LNCS, Springer (approved) and indexed by relevant databases.

The history of ruSMART dates back to 2008. Today it is an established conference featuring proceedings published by Springer LNCS.

The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.
