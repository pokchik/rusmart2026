Manuscript Submission

Manuscript Submission — ruSMART 2026
Submission for ruSMART 2026 is open. Manuscripts are handled through the Microsoft CMT conference management system.
Submit Your Paper via Microsoft CMT »
Submission Guidelines
  • Papers must describe original, previously unpublished, complete research, not currently under review by another conference or journal.
  • Papers must be submitted electronically through the Microsoft CMT conference site.
  • The maximum size of papers should be 5000 words including tables and figures.
  • Please adhere to the formatting standard for a 12-page manuscript of Springer LNCS.
  • The cover page must contain an abstract of about 150 words, 3–5 keywords, name and affiliation of author(s), and the corresponding author's e-mail and postal address.
Camera-Ready Formatting

The camera ready manuscript for ruSMART 2026 MUST be formatted in accordance with Springer's LNCS requirements — Springer's LNCS guidelines.
